Practical compliance guide

Plastic EPR annual return checklist for Indian businesses

Published 26 November 2026 · EPRTrack

Before preparing your plastic EPR annual return, bring your packaging transactions, official target records and certificate information into a consistent, reviewable format. This guide is a working checklist for producers, importers, brand owners and their consultants—not an official filing form or a complete list of statutory requirements.

Portal update: CPCB's former plastic packaging portal states that it was discontinued from 28 June 2026 and its data moved to the Common EPR Portal. Use the current portal for the applicable filing process, deadlines and official records.

1. What documents and data should you organise?

In a December 2024 parliamentary response, the government explained that plastic EPR annual returns include information on packaging material procurement and plastic packaging sold. The precise reporting fields depend on your role, applicable rules and current portal instructions. The following is a recommended internal preparation list rather than a claim that each item is mandatory for everyone.

Company and registration detailsLegal name, registration reference, role and reporting year.

2. Prepare your packaging Excel file

Use a consistent unit, such as kilograms for packaging weight. If a weight is per unit rather than the total for a transaction, calculate the total using a documented SKU basis first. For example, 1,000 units with 0.02 kg of plastic packaging each represent 20 kg of packaging, or 0.02 tonnes. That arithmetic is not itself an EPR obligation calculation.

Keep invoice references as text where leading zeroes matter. Record the financial year separately and retain evidence for category classifications. Repeated invoice references can represent legitimate product lines; investigate them rather than deleting every repeated reference.

3. Reconcile your EPR certificate register

Compare your internal certificate records with the corresponding official portal information. Do not treat a purchase invoice as proof that the corresponding EPR certificate has been transferred or credited. Review official status, issuer, category, period and quantity before relying on a record.

For a standardised CSV comparison, prepare both registers using the same four columns: reference, category, FY and quantity in tonnes. EPRTrack can flag duplicate keys, missing references and quantity differences. It does not verify certificates against CPCB or determine eligibility.

4. Review unresolved exceptions before preparing the return

  • Missing or invalid packaging weights and units.
  • Repeated invoice or certificate references that have not been reviewed.
  • Transactions assigned to the wrong financial year or packaging category.
  • Differences between internal quantities and official records.
  • Pending certificates or transfers not reflected in official credited quantities.

Document who reviewed each exception and why it was resolved. Keep the original files and a reviewed copy. Do not include pending evidence as confirmed fulfilment.

5. Keep a company-wise working report

Consultants managing multiple clients should maintain separate workspaces by company, financial year, role and category. EPRTrack stores the latest saved analyzer summary, entered targets and evidence reference records and can export an internal working CSV. It does not consolidate a full annual invoice ledger or generate a portal-ready statutory return yet.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I file the plastic EPR annual return?

Use CPCB's Common EPR Portal and follow the current instructions applicable to your registration and reporting year. EPRTrack does not submit returns.

What is the filing deadline?

Check the current notices in the official portal for your role and financial year. Filing windows and extensions can change, and this guide does not assert a current deadline.

Can I verify a certificate using an Excel comparison?

No. A comparison highlights inconsistencies between the two files you provide. Verify the actual certificate status and applicability through official records.

Is EPRTrack free?

All available features are free during the public beta. Use sample or non-sensitive records while security and privacy reviews are ongoing.
