Privacy Policy

Public beta data handling notice. Use sample or non-sensitive data while security and privacy reviews are ongoing.

The public calculator does not require PAN, GST documents or original compliance documents. The analyzer and reconciliation tools process uploaded files in your browser; saving an analysis sends the selected company and summary (including the file name) to the server. Account information, company details, manually entered compliance records, evidence references and tasks are stored in the application database. Do not enter confidential documents, portal credentials or personal data in free-text fields during beta.

Account deletion, retention and data recovery workflows are not yet self-service. This notice is not a completed legal privacy policy.
