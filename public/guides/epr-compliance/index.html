Plastic EPR Compliance Guide for India

A practical starting point for understanding roles, categories, recycling and reporting workflows.

Who is covered?

CPCB materials identify Producers, Importers and Brand Owners as PIBOs in the Plastic EPR framework. The official portal also describes Plastic Waste Processors and registration/annual-return modules.

What should a business track?

  • Financial year and applicable role
  • Plastic packaging quantities by category
  • Applicable target and recycling requirement
  • Recycled-content obligation where applicable
  • Evidence, certificates and reporting records

Portal update

CPCB states that the earlier Plastic EPR portal was discontinued from 28 June 2026 and the Common EPR Portal went live with migrated user data.
