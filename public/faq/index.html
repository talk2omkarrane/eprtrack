Plastic EPR FAQ

Common questions about EPR calculations and EPRTrack.

Is EPRTrack an official CPCB site?

No. It is independent software.

Does the calculator determine my legal obligation?

No. It is an indicative planning tool.

Why choose a role?

Producer, Importer and Brand Owner obligations can require different inputs and formulas.

What FY is used?

The current rule dataset is FY 2026–27 and displays its version and verification date.

EPRTrack — independent EPR compliance software for India.

Not CPCB, MoEFCC, or an official filing service. Calculations are indicative and should be verified against current official requirements.
